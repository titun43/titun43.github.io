Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

At Lokhnath Technical ("we", "us", or "our"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile applications — Note Counter Pro and ExamVault — collect, use, and protect information when you use our services. By downloading or using our apps, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Short version: We do NOT sell your personal data. Note Counter Pro works fully offline and stores data only on your device. ExamVault collects minimal data (name, email, exam progress) needed to provide the service. We use Google AdMob to show ads, which is the only way we monetize these free apps.

1. Information We Collect

Note Counter Pro

  • Local storage only: Cash counting sessions, saved totals, and preferences are stored on your device's local storage.
  • No account required: The app works completely offline. No personal information is collected or transmitted.
  • Camera access: Used only when you grant permission, solely for the note-scanning feature. Images are processed on-device and never uploaded.

ExamVault

  • Account information: Name, email address, and password (encrypted) when you create an account.
  • Exam data: Your mock test attempts, scores, bookmarks, and progress — used to provide personalized recommendations.
  • Device information: Device model, OS version, app version, and unique device identifier for analytics and crash reporting.
  • Usage data: Which features you use, time spent, and interaction events — used to improve the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the apps and their features.
  • To authenticate your account (ExamVault only) and sync your progress across devices.
  • To show relevant advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • To analyze app usage and improve user experience.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, and security concerns.
  • To respond to your support requests and feedback.

3. Third-Party Services

Our apps use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob & Google Mobile Ads: Serves advertisements. May collect device identifiers, coarse location, and interaction data to serve relevant ads. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Anonymous usage analytics and crash reporting. Firebase Privacy.
  • Google Play Services: For app updates, in-app purchases, and authentication.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): For sending push notifications about new mock tests, features, or updates (ExamVault only).

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share data only in the following cases:

  • With service providers (like Google, Firebase) who help us operate the apps.
  • If required by law, court order, or government request.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale.
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or others.

5. Data Retention

For ExamVault, we retain your account data and exam progress for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing us at lkstudeoandcomputering112@gmail.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location (including users in India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and users in the EEA/UK under GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Withdraw consent to data processing at any time.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your data.
  • Export your data in a portable format.

To exercise these rights, email lkstudeoandcomputering112@gmail.com.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data — including encryption in transit (TLS/SSL), encrypted password storage (bcrypt hashing), and restricted internal access. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

9. Cookies & Tracking

The mobile apps do not use cookies. Our website (titun43.github.io) may use standard web analytics cookies. AdMob may use device identifiers and advertising ID to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device's Google Settings → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization."
